Controlled lifecycle

Each state has a deliberate next step.

PulseBoard keeps incident transitions explicit rather than allowing arbitrary status changes. That makes the workflow easier to reason about and test.

01 Open

A newly reported issue awaiting active investigation.

02 Investigating

The owner is identifying cause, scope, and response.

03 Mitigated

Immediate impact is reduced while final resolution continues.

04 Resolved

The incident is closed after recovery or remediation.
